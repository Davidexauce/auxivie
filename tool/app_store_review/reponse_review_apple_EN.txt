3) App purpose and target audience

Display name on device: Aidalia.

Aidalia (Auxivie) is a French-language marketplace that connects families with home-care / domestic help professionals (“aide à domicile”). Families can search for professionals, manage multi-day reservations, exchange in-app messages, and pay through Stripe. Professionals can manage profile-related data, availability, bookings, and messaging.

Target audience: families seeking home assistance and independent professionals offering those services, primarily in France.

Problem solved: simplifying discovery, booking coordination, in-app communication, and secure payment for home-care arrangements.
